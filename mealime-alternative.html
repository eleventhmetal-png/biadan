Common questions

Can I import my Mealime recipes?

Mealime doesn't offer exports, but you're not stuck. Clip any recipe page with a web clipper, or use a third-party tool to pull your saved recipes out. Bíadán supports bulk import, so once you've got them, they come in from a file or spreadsheet.

Does Bíadán work with any grocery store?

Bíadán builds your list and takes you to the store's site. If you shop online, you log in with them. If you'd rather not, print the list and shop in person.

Is Bíadán free?

Yes. The weeknight loop is free forever: week plan, preferences and exclusions, swaps, merged grocery list with check-offs for any store, pantry-aware planning, cooking view, favorites, recipe import, list sharing, meal history, high-altitude guidance, manual store links, export, and print. Pro is $4.99 a month or $34.99 a year.

What sets Bíadán apart from other meal planning apps?

It's a meal planner and grocery list in one, with hands-free cooking mode, a built-in catalog including a curated mid-century recipe collection, plus Spoonacular recipes, nutrition info, clean printing from phone or computer, and online shopping.
